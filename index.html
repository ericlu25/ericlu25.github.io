About

Hi there! My name is Feiyu Lu. I am a Research Scientist in the Global Technology Applied Research Group at JPMorgan Chase, managed by Prof. Blair MacIntyre. I obtained my Ph.D. degree from Virginia Tech in May 2023. I was a proud member of the 3D Interaction Group directed by Prof. Doug A. Bowman and the Center for Human-Computer Interaction (CHCI) at Virginia Tech. I also worked as a Research Scientist Intern at Meta Reality Labs Research in the summer of 2021 and 2022, mentored by Dr. Yan Xu. Prior to joining VT, I was an undergraduate research assistant advised by Prof. Hai-Ning Liang. My research interests lie broadly in the intersections of Augmented/Virtual/Mixed Reality (AR/VR/MR), 3D User Interfaces (3DUI), Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and Machine Learning (ML). I have published full papers in premiere venues such as IEEEVR, ISMAR, TVCG, and ACM CHI, SUI.
